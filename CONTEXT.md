# Daggerheart Character Sheet

A digital version of the official two-page Daggerheart character sheet: an editable form beside a live, interactive reproduction of the sheet.

## Language

**Class**:
A character's role (e.g. Guardian). Supplies the sheet's name, domains, starting stats, Hope feature, class feature, questions and suggestions.

**Subclass**:
A specialisation of a Class (e.g. Stalwart, Vengeance), with Foundation, Specialization and Mastery features.

**Heritage**:
The character's Ancestry plus Community, written as one line (e.g. "Human Highborne"). An Ancestry may be mixed from two.

**Trait**:
One of six scores (Agility, Strength, Finesse, Instinct, Presence, Knowledge). The effective value is the creation spread, plus level-up picks, plus armor and active-weapon modifiers.

**Mark**:
The circle on a Trait showing it was raised by a level-up and cannot be raised again until marks are cleared (at levels 5 and 8). Derived from level-up picks, with a manual override.

**Tier**:
A band of levels: Tier 1 is level 1, Tier 2 is levels 2–4, Tier 3 is levels 5–7, Tier 4 is levels 8–10. Each Tier from 2 up has an achievement and a set of level-up options.

**Level-up pick**:
One ticked option box in a Tier's list. Each level-up grants two picks. Box counts per option are hard limits. The per-level allowance is a warning only. Earlier-tier options stay available in later tiers.

**Experience**:
A named +2 bonus the player can spend Hope on. Level-up picks of "+1 to two Experiences" target specific Experiences.

**Slot**:
A tickable box for HP, Stress or Armor. The marked count is stored as entered and only clamped when displayed.

**Damage threshold**:
The Minor and Major values (armor base thresholds plus level) that decide how many HP a hit marks.

**Suggested value**:
A default a selection pre-fills (class traits, armor, weapons).

**Override**:
A field the user edited by hand. Later selection changes never overwrite it. A reset-to-suggested control restores it.

**Adjustment**:
A stored manual ± on a derived stat (Evasion, thresholds, Armor score, HP, Stress, Proficiency), for homebrew or magic items.
